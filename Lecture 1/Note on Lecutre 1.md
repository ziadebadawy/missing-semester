## What is the Shell?
Computers have a bunch of interfaces you can use to interact with the computer. <u>**G**raphical **U**ser **I**nterfaces</u> (GUIs), where you move your mouse around to interact, are the most common and widely spread example of interfaces. Their popularity stems from ease of use, visual representation.

We also have other types of interfaces that are more recently developed, such as **A**gentic **U**ser **I**nterfaces (AUIs), **V**oice **U**ser **I**nterfaces (VUIs), **A**ugmented/**V**irtual **R**eality **I**nterfaces (AR/VR). 

All of these interfaces are essentially a way for us, the human, to interact with our machine. However, most of these interfaces are specialized by their manufacturer<sup>(sometimes referred to as the author)</sup> to do what they had in mind when they created the interface.
